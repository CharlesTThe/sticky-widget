The choice

Two ways to buy back the same density

Both routes recover roughly the same trailer fill. They differ in what they cost to reverse. Consolidation is a property decision and a slow one; densification is a planning decision we can unwind inside a quarter.

  • Eleven sub-scale sites account for 9% of volume and 21% of fixed network cost.
  • Meridian Foods is the anchor customer on four of those eleven lanes.
  • Neither option changes the committed delivery window for any customer.
  • Both assume the Rotterdam sortation upgrade lands on schedule.

Option A

Hub consolidation

Close the eleven sub-scale sites and re-point their volume onto the six regional hubs.

4.1m eur annual run-rate · 18 months to unwind

Option B

Spoke densification

Keep the footprint, halve the departure frequency, and hold volume back for fuller trailers.

3.6m eur annual run-rate · 1 quarter to unwind
